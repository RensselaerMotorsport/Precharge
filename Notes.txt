Precharge V3.0 with stm32

ADC:
Using adc in scan conversion mode triggered by a timer with DMA
Triggered by timer 1 Trigger out

IN1: Inverter side sample pin
IN5: Battery side sample pin

CAN Bus:
Bus configured at 500kbps
Data sent x100 for 2 decimal places
********************************************************************
* battVolt High * battVolt Low * invVolt High* invVolt Low * State *
********************************************************************

States:
0: idle, not signaled to precharge
1: charging, precharge relay closed
2: running, precharge completed and +Air closed
3: battery fault, no battery present
4: WDT fault, processor reset due to watchdog timer
